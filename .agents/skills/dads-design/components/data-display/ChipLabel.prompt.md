Chip / tag — categorise or label content. Choose `variant` for emphasis and `color` for meaning.

```jsx
<ChipLabel variant="fill" color="blue">新着</ChipLabel>
<ChipLabel variant="filled-outline" color="green">受付中</ChipLabel>
<ChipLabel variant="outline" color="gray">下書き</ChipLabel>
```

- `variant`: `text` · `outline` · `filled-outline` · `fill`.
- `color`: 11 options (gray, blue, light-blue, cyan, green, lime, yellow, orange, red, magenta, purple).
